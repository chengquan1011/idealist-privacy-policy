This Privacy Policy explains how Quan Cheng (“we”) handles information when you use the Idealist mobile app and related email-link and policy pages (together, the “Service”). Idealist is currently provided without commercial or advertising purposes.

1. Information we handle

Account information

When you create an account or sign in, we process your email address and authentication credentials through Supabase Auth. Supabase manages the account password for authentication; we do not use it for advertising. Your Idealist profile may include your display name, username/handle, optional headline, and avatar.

Content you provide

We store the titles, descriptions, links, categories, and images you submit as original ideas, discoveries, or research, including drafts. If you send a suggestion or contact support, we process the message and the account details needed to respond.

  • Drafts: draft posts and their media are restricted to the account that owns them.
  • Published content: published posts and the public profile fields listed above are visible to other Idealist users. Published post images are delivered through time-limited signed links (currently one hour); anyone who obtains an unexpired link may be able to view that image. People may also save, screenshot, or share content they can see.
  • Avatars: profile avatars are stored in a public image bucket because they are displayed on public profiles.

Interactions and guest use

We process favorites, likes or other reactions, firewood interactions, and post-view counts to operate the corresponding features. If you interact without signing in, the app creates a random, persistent viewer identifier on your device and uses it to associate guest interactions; it is not an advertising ID.

Reports, blocking, and moderation

When you report a post or author, we process the selected reason, the reported post or account identifier, and the report’s review status so the administrator can investigate. Report counts may help prioritize the manual review queue; reports do not automatically hide content. If you block an author, we store that blocking relationship to filter their content from your feeds and favorites. We also store system notices shown in the app, such as warnings, content-removal notices, or publishing restrictions, and whether you have read them.

Automated content screening

Before an Original or Discovery post is published, its title, text, and attached images are screened using OpenAI’s Moderation API. OpenAI returns moderation categories and scores. Content flagged for review stays private and is not published unless the administrator approves it. We store the screening outcome and category signals in a restricted moderation record. Research posts added by the administrator are not part of this user-submission screening flow.

Device and local app data

The app stores session information, language preference, recent sign-in email addresses, and a viewer identifier on your device. Images may also be cached locally to display them more smoothly. The sign-in screen currently offers “Remember Password” and has it selected by default. When enabled, remembered credentials and session values use iOS secure storage when available; if secure storage is unavailable, the app falls back to files in its private app storage. That fallback is not equivalent to Keychain protection. You can turn off “Remember Password,” and should do so on a shared or untrusted device.

The app does not request precise location or access contacts. When you choose a photo or use the camera, the selected image is used for the profile or post you are editing.

Technical and service data

Our infrastructure providers may receive IP address, request time, app/operating-system and runtime details, error or security logs, and other technical information needed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the Service. When the app checks for over-the-air updates, Expo says it may receive the device operating system and randomized update tokens; its services may also process network and diagnostic data under its own policy. We do not currently integrate an in-app advertising or third-party analytics SDK.

2. How we use information

  • Create and secure accounts; verify email addresses; sign users in; and send requested password-reset or account emails.
  • Store drafts, publish content, display profiles and feeds, and provide favorites, reactions, firewood, and view counts.
  • Screen submitted Original and Discovery content for safety, prioritize reports for manual review, and administer warnings, content decisions, or publishing restrictions.
  • Respond to user questions and suggestions, maintain the Service, investigate abuse or security issues, and fix errors.
  • Meet legal obligations and protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the Service.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles. Idealist currently does not provide AI generation, voice recording/transcription, investment, recharge, or payment features; we therefore do not collect voice recordings or payment-card details through those roadmap concepts.

3. When information is shared

We share information only as needed to run the Service, at your direction (for example, when you publish content), or as required by law:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, storage, and server functions.
  • Resend delivers transactional account emails, such as confirmation and password-reset messages. It receives the recipient email address and the information needed to deliver the requested email.
  • OpenAI processes the title, text, and images of Original and Discovery submissions through its Moderation API for safety screening. OpenAI says API data is not used to train or improve its models unless the customer opts in; its abuse-monitoring logs may retain API inputs, outputs, and related metadata for up to 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect its services or others. See OpenAI API data controls.
  • Expo / EAS delivers app updates and related technical services.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the privacy and email-callback pages. When you open an email link, the browser may contact GitHub Pages and Supabase with link parameters needed to complete the callback. The static pages are not intended to run advertising or analytics; GitHub may process ordinary web-request information under its own policies.
  • Apple provides the iOS operating system and App Store distribution and may process information under Apple’s own policies.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. They may use infrastructure in countries other than yours. Provider notices: Supabase, Resend, OpenAI, Expo, GitHub, and Apple.

4. Public content and security

Think carefully before publishing personal information. Other users may copy or redistribute public content. Draft post media is private and is not served through permanent public links. Published post media is stored in a private bucket and served using signed, time-limited links. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, and a person who receives a signed link can use it until it expires.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep information while needed to provide the Service, maintain an account and its content, address security issues, or meet legal obligations. You may delete eligible posts in the app. You can request account deletion from the Delete Account control on the Personal Information page in the app, or contact bill-labs@outlook.com from the account email address. Account deletion removes the account, drafts, published posts, and related media, subject to information we must retain for legal, security, dispute-resolution, or backup purposes. We may ask you to verify ownership. Copies already downloaded or screenshotted by other users, or retained in service-provider backups, may not be immediately removable.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

You can edit profile details, choose whether to publish content, remove eligible posts, and disable “Remember Password.” Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us below; we may verify your identity and will respond as required by applicable law.

7. Children

Idealist is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

8. International processing

Our providers may process or store information in countries where they or their infrastructure operate, which may have data-protection laws different from those in your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes or as required by law. We will revise the effective date and provide a notice where appropriate. Questions, rights requests, and deletion requests can be sent to bill-labs@outlook.com.

Before October 1, 2026, a database permission could allow email addresses stored in profile records to be retrieved through the public profile API. On October 1, 2026, we removed public and ordinary authenticated access to that profile email field; email addresses are no longer returned by the profile API. We have not determined whether anyone accessed or retained profile email data before the permission was changed. If you have questions, contact us at bill-labs@outlook.com.

Previously public post-image links

On October 1, 2026, we changed post-image storage so drafts are private and published images require expiring signed links. Existing public links are now denied by the storage access policy. We cannot recall copies downloaded or shared before that change, and copies retained in third-party caches may remain available until those caches expire.

本隐私政策说明 Quan Cheng（“我们”）在你使用 Idealist 移动应用及相关邮件链接页面和隐私政策页面（统称“服务”）时如何处理信息。目前提供 Idealist 并非出于商业或广告目的。

1. 我们处理的信息

账号信息

创建账号或登录时，我们通过 Supabase Auth 处理你的邮箱地址和身份验证凭证。Supabase 管理用于身份验证的账号密码；我们不会将其用于广告。Idealist 个人资料可能包括昵称、用户名/账号名、可选的个人简介和头像。

你提供的内容

我们会存储你作为原创、见闻或研究内容提交的标题、描述、链接、分类和图片，也包括草稿。你提交建议或联系支持时，我们会处理消息内容以及回复所需的账号信息。

  • 草稿：草稿及其媒体文件仅允许所属账号访问。
  • 已发布内容：已发布帖子以及上文列出的公开个人资料字段会展示给其他 Idealist 用户。已发布帖子的图片通过限时签名链接提供（目前有效期为一小时）；获得未过期链接的人可能能够查看图片。其他人也可能保存、截图或分享其可见内容。
  • 头像：个人资料头像存放于公开图片存储空间，因为头像会显示在公开个人资料中。

互动和游客使用

为提供相应功能，我们会处理收藏、点赞或其他互动、拾柴互动以及帖子浏览计数。未登录时，应用会在设备上生成一个随机且持久的访客标识，用来关联游客互动；它不是广告标识符。

举报、屏蔽和内容审核

当你举报帖子或作者时，我们会处理你选择的原因、被举报的帖子或账号标识，以及举报的审核状态，以便管理员调查处理。举报数量可能用于安排人工审核优先级；举报本身不会自动隐藏内容。你屏蔽作者后，我们会保存屏蔽关系，以便在你的信息流和收藏中屏蔽其内容。我们也会保存应用内展示的系统通知及其已读状态，例如警告、内容处理通知或发布限制通知。

自动内容筛查

原创或见闻帖子发布前，其标题、文字和所附图片会通过 OpenAI 的 Moderation API 进行安全筛查。OpenAI 会返回内容类别和分数。被标记需复核的内容会保持私有，在管理员批准前不会发布。我们会在受限的审核记录中保存筛查结果和类别信号。管理员添加的研究内容不属于此用户投稿筛查流程。

设备和应用本地数据

应用会在设备本地保存会话信息、语言偏好、最近登录使用的邮箱地址和访客标识。为减少图片闪烁，图片也可能缓存在本地。登录页目前提供“记住密码”选项，且默认选中。启用时，记住的凭据和会话信息会在可用时使用 iOS 安全存储；若安全存储不可用，应用会回退到应用私有目录中的文件。此回退方式不等同于 iOS 钥匙串保护。你可以关闭“记住密码”；在共享或不可信设备上应关闭此选项。

应用不会请求精确位置或访问通讯录。当你选择照片或使用相机时，所选图片会用于你正在编辑的个人资料或帖子。

技术和服务数据

我们的基础设施服务商可能会收到提供、保护和排查服务所需的 IP 地址、请求时间、应用/操作系统及运行环境信息、错误或安全日志等技术信息。应用检查无线更新时，Expo 表示可能会收到设备操作系统信息和随机生成的更新令牌；其服务也可能根据自己的政策处理网络和诊断数据。目前应用未集成应用内广告或第三方分析 SDK。
